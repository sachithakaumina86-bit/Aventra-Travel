## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-touch bespoke travel, catering to affluent global travelers, honeymooners, private collectors, and architectural enthusiasts seeking private Ceylon expeditions. The aesthetic mirrors the quiet prestige of heritage tea bungalows, bespoke coastal villas, and high-end editorial periodicals like *Condé Nast Traveler* and *Aman Private Escapes*.

The visual language balances **Quiet Luxury Editorial** with **Organic Modernism**:
- **Tactile Serenity:** Warm ivory and soft sand substrates replace stark digital whites, establishing an unhurried, grounded ambiance reminiscent of archival heavy-stock travel journals.
- **Micro-Gilded Accents:** Refined hairline strokes and subtle champagne gold indicators anchor high-contrast emerald landscapes without ostentation.
- **Cinematic Pacing:** Ample negative space pairs with asymmetrical editorial grid blocks, allowing lush imagery of misty hill country tea estates, pristine southern coastlines, and Sigiriya monoliths to function as architectural anchors.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the untamed lushness of Sri Lanka’s central highlands and deep coastal waters, punctuated by antique brass and sunlight on Ceylon tea leaves.

- **Primary (`#064E3B` - Deep Emerald):** The foundational brand color, conveying heritage, primeval rainforests, and exclusivity. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key banners, and impactful structural headings.
- **Deep Forest (`#022C22`):** Used for deep contrasting footers, dark mode moments, modal scrims, and intense framing elements.
- **Secondary (`#D6B56D` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for luxury badges, active state indicators, hairline ornamental dividers, concierge tags, and bespoke iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E` - Ocean Teal):** Evokes the Indian Ocean surrounding the southern coast; applied to experiential tags, marine excursion highlights, and secondary interactive accents.
- **Neutral Backgrounds (`#FAF9F5` Warm Ivory & `#F0E9DC` Soft Sand):** The primary canvas surfaces. Warm Ivory forms the luminous viewport ground, while Soft Sand creates structural nesting panels, card backgrounds, and architectural content compartments.
- **Text Hierarchies:** Deep Slate (`#18231F`) delivers high-contrast readability with a softer, richer warmth than pure black. Muted Olive (`#7B847F`) provides balanced metadata, captions, and secondary narrative body.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs classical European editorial serif stature with hyper-functional, precise sans-serif utility:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke luxury and literary gravitas. Display titles use slight negative tracking for tighter editorial impact. Hero headers often pair with subtle italics for evocative travel phrasing (e.g., *“The Uncharted Province”*).
- **Body & Captions (Inter):** Set at a light to regular weight with generous line height (1.6 to 1.7) to allow effortless scanning of long-form itineraries, villa specifications, and cultural stories.
- **Labels & Navigation (Inter Uppercase):** All top-tier navigation links, category teasers, badge metrics, and section trackers are styled in uppercase with tracking spanning `0.12em` to `0.16em` to maintain architectural alignment with gilded divider lines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors fine art publishing: expansive breathing room, precise vertical alignment, and structured visual tension.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid grid anchored within a max content boundary of `1440px`. Column gutters remain spacious at `2.5rem`, allowing alternating 7/5 or 8/4 column pairings for editorial storytelling (narrative copy pinned beside a full-bleed portrait capture).
  - **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` page margins.
  - **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact flow with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Sections:** Hero narratives feature offset imagery overlapping modular ivory background tiles by `2rem` to `3rem`.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors leverage a 4px/8px micro-system, while structural section transitions utilize generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to preserve a calm, uncrowded journey.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes materiality, layered physical depth, and subtle light refraction over harsh drop shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders:** Panels, cards, and modal components rely primarily on 1px hairline outlines tinted in Champagne Gold at 25% opacity (`rgba(214, 181, 109, 0.25)`) or soft sand boundaries (`rgba(24, 35, 31, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Nesting:** Hierarchy is communicated by elevating Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F5`) cards over Soft Sand (`#F0E9DC`) containers, or stacking Sand inquiry cards against deep photo backgrounds.
- **Ambient Diffused Shadows:** Floating booking navigation bars and curated itinerary preview drawers utilize extremely wide, low-density shadows with a deep green undertone: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Frosted Glassmorphism:** Sticky navigation bars, itinerary quick-filters, and image overlay tags use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with a 85% semi-translucent Warm Ivory or 60% Deep Forest tint, creating translucent layers that hint at imagery underneath.

## Shapes

The geometric personality is architectural and tailored:

- **Structural Forms:** Cards, image frames, text containers, and interactive dropdowns employ **Soft** geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px`), referencing stone monoliths, classical colonial architraves, and tailored travel documents.
- **Exceptions (Pill Badges & Buttons):** Status badges, curatorial collection chips, and micro-tags utilize full pill radii (`rounded-full`) to offer an organic, tactile contrast against rectilinear editorial photo frames.
- **Divider Elements:** Tailored 1px hairline lines, accented by diamond or subtle circular gold terminal points, partition discrete narrative chapters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Curated Journey / Reserve):** Solid Deep Emerald (`#064E3B`) with Warm Ivory typography in uppercase Inter (`nav-link` styling). Generous horizontal padding (`1.75rem`), vertical padding (`0.875rem`). Hover transitions smoothly to Deep Forest (`#022C22`) with an ambient champagne drop glow.
- **Secondary Action (Discover More):** Transparent background with a 1px Champagne Gold (`#D6B56D`) border and `#064E3B` or `#FAF9F5` typography. Hover initiates a gilded fill with slate text.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Uppercase tracked label followed by a bespoke hairline arrow (`→`). Hover animates the arrow 4px forward with an emerald or gold bottom underline reveal.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Category Pills:** Semi-transparent Soft Sand (`#F0E9DC`) background with Deep Emerald text, framed by a 1px gold hairline. Height `28px`, uppercase typography with `0.12em` tracking.
- **Status Tags:** Deep Forest (`#022C22`) pill carrying gold micro-text for ultra-exclusive packages (e.g., “PRIVATE JET ONLY”, “TEA TRAIN HERITAGE”).

### Cards
- **Destination & Expedition Cards:** Pure White or Warm Ivory containers with 4px corner radii. Imagery occupies a prominent 4:5 or 16:10 aspect ratio framed by a hairline border (`rgba(214, 181, 109, 0.2)`). Card footer features Playfair Display headline, itinerary duration in Muted Olive, and price tier aligned right. Hover triggers a subtle image scale (1.03x) within an overflow-hidden mask.

### Input Fields & Inquiry Forms
- **Luxury Inquiry Fields:** Minimalist architectural form styling. Flat Warm Ivory surface, floating uppercase label in Muted Olive, and a bottom border in `1.5px` muted gold (`rgba(214, 181, 109, 0.4)`). Upon focus, border shifts to vibrant Deep Emerald (`#064E3B`) with zero default browser outlines.
- **Date & Guest Selectors:** Custom segmented input panels displaying calendar dates with serif numerals, evoking bespoke boarding passes.

### Lists & Itinerary Steppers
- **Day-by-Day Timeline:** Vertical 1px dashed Champagne Gold track. Milestone nodes feature 8px solid emerald circles centered within a 16px soft sand ring. Content modules host the day indicator in Playfair Display accompanied by expandable excursion specifics.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (checkbox) and circular (radio) controls with 1px gold micro-borders. Checked states fill with Deep Emerald containing a crisp Warm Ivory check icon or brass center dot.
