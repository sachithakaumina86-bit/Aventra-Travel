Ethos & Preservation

The AVENTRA Code of Ethical Stewardship

Luxury travel in Sri Lanka must leave the island more resilient than we found it. We measure true luxury not merely by the thread-count of our bungalows, but by the dignity afforded to the ecosystems and heritage communities we visit.

nest_eco_leaf

Zero Captivity Interaction Policy

AVENTRA categorically refuses rides, bathing spectacles, or staged interactions with captive elephants. We encounter wildlife exclusively in open national parks, buffer sanctuaries, and verified research sanctuaries at ethical observation distances.

verified Audited Wildlife Protocol

Direct Community Equity

Twelve percent of every experience fee flows straight into local trusts: from educational stipends for tea plucker families in Bogawantalawa to funding GPS satellite monitoring collars for the Sri Lankan Leopard Project.

Carbon-Neutral Expeditions

All private overland transfers and seaplane movements are verified and offset in partnership with the Sinharaja Rainforest Buffer Reforestation Initiative, restoring native canopy trees for every mile traveled.
